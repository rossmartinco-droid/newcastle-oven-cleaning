## Newcastle Great Park — new-build kitchens are most of what I do here

Great Park is one of the largest planned developments in Newcastle, and the housing stock is overwhelmingly newer than 20 years old. That has a specific implication for oven cleaning: most kitchens were installed with high-spec integrated appliances (often Neff, Bosch, or Siemens), and many haven't had a proper professional clean since the day they were fitted.

## Typical Great Park job

A standard Great Park booking is a tall housing unit with a stacked double oven and a separate combi grill, plus an induction hob, plus an integrated extractor. The combi grill in particular tends to be neglected — owners use it occasionally, never clean inside the housing frame, and grease builds up out of sight. A first deep clean on a 5–10 year-old Great Park kitchen usually pulls out genuinely impressive volumes of accumulated residue.
