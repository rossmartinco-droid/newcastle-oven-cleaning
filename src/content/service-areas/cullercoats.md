## Cullercoats — small, coastal, distinct from Tynemouth and Whitley Bay

Cullercoats is its own pocket, sandwiched between Tynemouth to the south and Whitley Bay to the north. The original fishing village around the bay is mostly older cottages and converted properties; back from the seafront the housing is 1930s semis and a small amount of newer infill. Demographic skews older, established, owner-occupied.

## Typical job profile

Smaller kitchens generally — single ovens dominate, with a fair share of integrated double ovens in the inland streets. Hob and extractor add-ons are common.

## Streets

Beverley Terrace, John Street, Front Street, Eleanor Street, the streets running back toward Hillheads Road and the Marden border.

## Salt-air consideration

Same as Tynemouth — properties within 200m of the bay need extractor filter cleans more often. I flag this when I leave a job.

## Drive and access

About 30 minutes from Fenham via the Coast Road. Parking is straightforward on most residential streets.
