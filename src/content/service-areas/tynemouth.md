## Tynemouth — coastal, affluent, distinct

Tynemouth village is its own thing — separate in feel and demographic from the wider North Tyneside borough. The streets around the Priory, Front Street, and the seafront terraces are predominantly long-term homeowners, retirees, and a growing population of London/Edinburgh second-homers. Inland, Manor Road and Percy Park Road have the larger detached houses with the bigger kitchens and the bigger appliances.

## Salt air matters here

A specific Tynemouth-only consideration: salt-laden coastal air affects extractor filters faster than inland. Properties within 200m of the seafront tend to need extractor cleaning more often (every 9 months rather than annually), and hob trim and door hinges can show pitting earlier. I factor this into the recommended re-clean schedule when I leave a job.

## Streets I work on most

Front Street, Hotspur Street, Beverley Terrace (seafront end), Tynemouth Road, Percy Park Road, Manor Road, Allendale Place, Park Crescent, the streets around the Metro station.

## Typical job profile

Mix of single ovens (the smaller seafront flats), double ovens (most family houses), and a strong showing of range cookers in the inland streets. Hob and extractor add-ons are common — Tynemouth families tend to do the full kitchen at once rather than piecemeal.

## Drive and parking

About 30 minutes from Fenham via the Coast Road. Front Street and the immediate village centre have permit-controlled parking; I carry visitor permits and handle this myself. The inland residential streets are mostly off-street driveways.
