## Whitley Bay — the Monkseaton and Marden end specifically

Whitley Bay covers a wide area and varies a lot in demographic. I focus on the Monkseaton and Marden end — Hillheads Road, Marden Park, Monkseaton Drive, Brierdene Crescent, Rockcliffe Avenue, The Links — which is established residential, owner-occupied, mostly 1930s semis and bungalows. The town-centre flats area and the seafront short-let properties aren't really my target market.

## Typical job profile

Mostly single and double ovens, plus a notable contingent of bungalow-style integrated ovens (Bosch, Neff). Range cookers are less common here than in Gosforth or Ponteland, but a steady scattering exists in the larger Marden detached homes.

## Streets I work on most

Hillheads Road, Marden Park, Monkseaton Drive, Brierdene Crescent, Rockcliffe Avenue, The Links, Earsdon Road (Monkseaton end), Front Street Monkseaton, Cauldwell Lane.

## Demographic and demand

Two patterns. Long-term retired homeowners (the bulk of Monkseaton) tend to book yearly maintenance cleans, often pre-Christmas or pre-spring. Established families in the slightly larger detached homes book pre-Christmas + a top-up in May. End-of-tenancy work is rare here — Monkseaton is overwhelmingly owner-occupied.

## Drive and access

About 30 minutes from Fenham via the Coast Road. Parking is universally off-street driveways in the residential pockets — no permit zones away from the seafront.
