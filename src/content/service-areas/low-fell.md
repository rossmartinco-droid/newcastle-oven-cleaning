## Low Fell specifically — not generic Gateshead

Gateshead as a whole isn't my target market — most of the borough's housing stock is outside the affluent/elderly demographic I focus on. **Low Fell is the exception.** The streets running off Durham Road — Belle Vue Bank, Chowdene Bank, the Crest, Beaconsfield Road — are established family-home territory, mostly 1920s and 1930s semis with the occasional larger detached property and a steady share of range cookers.

## Typical job profile

A mix of single ovens (most common in the smaller semis), double ovens (the larger 1930s detached properties), and a notable range cooker contingent in the Chowdene and Eighton Banks streets. Hob and extractor add-ons are routine.

## Streets I work on most

Durham Road residential streets, Belle Vue Bank, Chowdene Bank, Eighton Banks, Beaconsfield Road, The Crest, Cannon Street.

## Drive and access

About 15 minutes from Fenham via the Tyne Bridge. Easy parking — off-street driveways throughout the residential streets.

## Why I treat Low Fell as its own area, not Gateshead generally

Gateshead is large and varied. Most of it (Felling, Wallsend boundary, Bensham, Dunston) is council-stock-heavy or working-class residential — different demographic, different appliance mix, different price sensitivity. Low Fell sits inside Gateshead administratively but is functionally a Newcastle commuter suburb in everything except postcode. That's why it gets its own page and the rest of Gateshead doesn't.
