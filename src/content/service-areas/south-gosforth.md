## South Gosforth specifically

South Gosforth is older and denser than Gosforth proper. The streets around the Metro station — Station Road, Church Road, Lyndhurst Avenue — are mostly Victorian terraces and Edwardian semis, often with modernised kitchens that have been refitted in the last 15–20 years. The customer profile is a mix: long-term retired residents who've owned the house since the 80s, plus younger professional families moving in for the Gosforth school catchments.

## Typical job profile

Most South Gosforth bookings are single or double ovens — fewer range cookers than in Gosforth proper, but a reasonable scattering of Stoves and Belling installations in the larger semis. Hob and extractor add-ons are popular here because of the older properties' tendency to have separate gas hobs rather than range cookers.

## Streets I work most often

Station Road, Church Road, Haddricks Mill Road, Lyndhurst Avenue, the south end of West Avenue, and the connecting streets around Jesmond Vale. Parking is on-street but unrestricted in most of the area.

## Annual demand

October–November pre-Christmas, then a smaller bump in May. End-of-tenancy work is rarer here than in Jesmond — South Gosforth is mostly owner-occupied.
