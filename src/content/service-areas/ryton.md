## Ryton — established west-of-Newcastle village

Ryton is a residential village 7 miles west of central Newcastle. It's mostly two halves — Old Ryton around Holy Cross Church with the older village stock, and the newer 1980s–90s estate developments stretching out toward Crawcrook. Demographic is overwhelmingly long-term residents — the kind of customer base where word-of-mouth carries more weight than search.

## Typical job profile

Mostly single and double ovens, plus a notable contingent of range cookers in the larger detached properties. Hob and extractor add-ons are routine — Ryton kitchens tend to be done as full bookings rather than piecemeal.

## Streets I work on most

Main Road, The Crescent, Old Ryton village streets, Hedgefield Avenue, Holburn Lane, plus the Crawcrook border streets.

About 18 minutes from Fenham via the A695. Easy parking.
