## Wideopen

Wideopen sits 5 miles north of Newcastle, on the boundary of Newcastle Great Park. The village mixes older stock with the 1970s–80s family estates that built out as the area grew. Demographic is established families plus a steady flow of younger arrivals attracted by the Great Park boundary and school catchments.

## Typical job profile

Mostly single and double ovens. Some range cookers in the larger detached properties on Coach Lane and Brunton Lane. Hob and extractor add-ons routine.
