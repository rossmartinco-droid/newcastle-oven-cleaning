## High Heaton specifically — not generic Heaton

Heaton is two areas in one. The southern end, around Chillingham Road and the streets running toward Byker, is Tyneside-flat territory — high tenant churn, end-of-tenancy work dominated by letting agents. **High Heaton is different.** It's almost entirely family homes, owner-occupied, mostly 1920s and 1930s semis, with established residents and the kind of consistent annual oven-cleaning demand you'd expect from an affluent residential pocket.

## Streets and demand

Heaton Park Road, Newton Road, Cartington Terrace, Rothbury Terrace, Heaton Hall Road and The Spinney are the streets I work on most. The houses tend to have:

- A standard built-in single oven plus four-burner gas hob (most common)
- Increasingly, integrated double ovens — often Neff or Bosch — with combi grills
- A smaller number of range cookers (Rangemaster, Stoves) in the larger detached properties

Annual demand peaks pre-Christmas and again in May (post-Easter spring clean). Repeat bookings make up the majority — most High Heaton customers stick with one oven cleaner once they find someone reliable.

## Access and parking

High Heaton is mostly off-street parking on driveways, no permit zones. Easy access. The streets are wide enough for compact equipment and there are very few stairs to navigate (most houses are ground-floor kitchens).

## Why I work this end of Heaton specifically

I'm a sole trader, not a high-volume operation. Targeting end-of-tenancy churn doesn't suit my model — it's price-sensitive, high-volume, low-margin work that suits the franchises better. High Heaton is the opposite: established homeowners, repeat business, willingness to pay for a thorough job done by a named person. That's where I focus.
