## Ponteland — affluent commuter village, range cookers a speciality

Ponteland is one of the wealthiest residential pockets in the North East and one of my higher-value areas. The housing mix runs from older village-centre cottages around Bell Villas and Coates Green up to the newer executive estates off Eastern Way and Callerton Lane. Almost every kitchen I work on in Ponteland has either a double oven or a full range cooker — single ovens are the exception, not the rule.

## Typical Ponteland appliance

Rangemaster Classic 110, Stoves Richmond, Belling Cookcentre, Smeg Concert, occasional Falcon and Lacanche in the higher-end properties. These take 3–4 hours on site, dismantled properly, racks and pan supports degreased, hob caps polished, doors taken apart where needed. Fixed price £160 regardless of width or condition.
