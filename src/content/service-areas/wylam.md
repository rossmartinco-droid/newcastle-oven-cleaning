## Wylam — small but distinctive affluent village

Wylam is a riverside village 9 miles west of Newcastle, sandwiched between Hexham and Ryton along the Tyne. Small population, but the demographic is overwhelmingly professional commuters and established homeowners — the kind of area where annual oven maintenance is taken for granted.

## Typical job profile

Mix of Victorian terraces (often with smaller, older kitchens but well-maintained appliances) and 1930s detached houses with double ovens or range cookers. The newer infill properties tend to have integrated double ovens.

## Streets I work on most

Main Road, Stephenson Terrace, Algernon Terrace, West Road (Wylam end), Falcon Terrace, the streets running down toward the river.

## Drive and access

About 20 minutes from Fenham via the A69. No parking restrictions on the residential streets.
